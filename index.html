How to Play

The goal: get yourself to the other side to win. Place fences to slow down your opponent's path and secure your own!

Locations on the board are referred to by their column letter and row number. For example, "A3" refers to the first column, 3rd row from the top.

Fences are placed between the squares. They'll start at the edge closer to A1, the TOP LEFT corner. So a horizontal "C2 Fence" will go above C2 and D2, visualized above.
